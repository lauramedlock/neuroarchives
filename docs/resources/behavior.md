# Behavior, video & pose datasets

Raw and annotated behavior video, pose-estimation ground truth, and strain-level phenotype compendia.

*11 resources. Generated from [`data/neuroscience_data_resources.csv`](../about/contributing.md) — edit the CSV, not this page.*

| Resource | Host (Region) | Species | Data type / technique | Level | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[MABe / CalMS21](https://sites.google.com/view/computational-behavior/our-datasets)** | Caltech (Anderson & Perona labs) + collaborators (USA) | Mouse; fly | Annotated social-behavior video, pose trajectories, and paired neural data | Behavior | Fully open | Multi-Agent Behavior benchmark: millions of annotated frames of mouse social interaction (CalMS21) and fly groups, designed for behavior-classification challenges. |
| **[Caltech Resident-Intruder Mouse dataset (CRIM13)](https://data.caltech.edu/records/8fqx2-7bh37)** | California Institute of Technology (USA) | Mouse | Synchronized top/side-view video of resident-intruder assays with frame-level action labels | Behavior | Fully open | 237 ten-minute videos annotated for 13 social-behavior categories; a long-standing reference for action recognition in rodents. |
| **[Rat 7M / DANNCE](https://github.com/spoonsso/dannce)** | Harvard Medical School / Duke (Ölveczky & Dunn labs) (USA) | Rat | 7 million frames of multi-camera video with motion-capture ground truth for 3D pose | Behavior | Fully open | The benchmark dataset for 3D markerless pose estimation in freely behaving rodents, paired with the DANNCE network. |
| **[DeepLabCut Model Zoo & benchmarks](https://www.mackenziemathislab.org/dlc-modelzoo)** | EPFL / Mathis lab (formerly Harvard) (Switzerland) | Mouse; horse; dog; NHP; fly; human; others | Labeled pose datasets and pretrained SuperAnimal models; Horse-10 out-of-domain benchmark | Behavior | Open source + open data | Pretrained pose models and the labeled datasets behind them, enabling markerless tracking without new annotation for many species. |
| **[SLEAP sample datasets](https://sleap.ai)** | Salk Institute / Princeton (Pereira, Murthy, Shaevitz) (USA) | Fly; mouse; bee; others | Multi-animal labeled pose datasets | Behavior | Open source, BSD | Reference datasets accompanying the SLEAP multi-animal tracking framework, including dense social-interaction recordings. |
| **[OpenMonkeyStudio / OpenMonkeyChallenge](http://openmonkeychallenge.com)** | University of Minnesota / Yale (Hayden lab) (USA) | Macaque; other primates | Multi-camera markerless 3D pose of freely moving macaques; ~100k annotated primate images | Behavior | Fully open | The principal open resource for primate pose estimation, spanning 26 species in the challenge dataset. |
| **[MacaquePose](http://www.pri.kyoto-u.ac.jp/datasets/macaquepose)** | RIKEN Center for Brain Science (Japan) | Macaque | ~13,000 manually keypoint-annotated macaque images in natural settings | Behavior | Fully open | Pose-annotation corpus used to train macaque-specific deep pose models. |
| **[Open Worm Movement Database](http://movement.openworm.org)** | MRC London Institute of Medical Sciences / OpenWorm (UK) | C. elegans | High-throughput worm tracking videos and extracted behavioral feature time series | Behavior | Fully open | Thousands of tracked worms across mutant strains with standardized Tierpsy features, enabling behavioral genetics at scale. |
| **[Mouse Phenome Database](https://phenome.jax.org)** | The Jackson Laboratory (USA) | Mouse | Strain-level behavioral, physiological and genetic phenotypes | Genes; behavior | Fully open | Curated cross-strain phenotype compendium (including many neurobehavioral assays) with genotype and QTL integration. |
| **[International Mouse Phenotyping Consortium (IMPC)](https://www.mousephenotype.org)** | IMPC / EMBL-EBI coordination (International) | Mouse | Standardized phenotyping of single-gene knockouts, incl. neurological and behavioral tests | Genes; behavior | Fully open | Systematic knockout-phenotype resource covering thousands of genes, with open-field, startle/PPI, and sensory assays plus brain imaging for selected lines. |
| **[Days of our Mice (DooM)](https://dandi.emberarchive.org/dandiset/000564/0.261015.2324)** | Salk Institute & Northwestern University (Pereira and Kennedy labs, who built SLEAP) with 15 contributing labs; hosted on the EMBER Archive (USA; International) | Mouse | Behavior video (587 MP4s); SLEAP pose tracking (.slp); human-scored behavior bouts; Parquet pose/annotation tables | Behavior | Open access, CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0; DOI 10.60533/ember-dandi.000564/0.261015.2324 | Cross-laboratory dataset of freely interacting mice: 587 videos from 15 labs with 71,870 human-annotated bouts spanning 33 behavior types, assembled by 48 authors across 25 institutions. These are the lab-contributed datasets of the MABe-2025 behavior-classification competition at the cleaned 2026-07-18 revision used to train the HiDRA classifier; the competition's other sources (MABe22, CalMS21, CRIM13) are published separately. Each video ships an MP4, a SLEAP .slp holding pose tracks and bouts, and the same pose and bouts in the competition Parquet format. 200.8 GB across 2,352 files. |
