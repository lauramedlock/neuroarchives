# Computational models, simulation & benchmarks

Model source code, simulation platforms, whole-brain simulators and benchmarks that score models against neural data.

*13 resources. Generated from [`data/neuroscience_data_resources.csv`](../about/contributing.md) — edit the CSV, not this page.*

| Resource | Host (Region) | Species | Data type / technique | Level | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[ModelDB](https://modeldb.science)** | Yale University SenseLab (USA) | All | Source code for published computational neuroscience models | Models | Fully open | The canonical archive of >1,800 published neuronal and network model implementations (NEURON, NEST, MATLAB, Python), linked to their papers. |
| **[Open Source Brain](https://www.opensourcebrain.org)** | UCL / Open Source Brain project (UK) | All | NeuroML/PyNN model sharing with in-browser 3D visualization and simulation | Models; cells; circuits | Open source | Platform for standardized, runnable and visualizable neuronal models — from single channels to cortical networks — with cloud execution. |
| **[NeuroML Database](https://neuroml-db.org)** | University of Texas San Antonio / NeuroML community (USA) | All | Curated NeuroML models with automatically computed electrophysiological characterizations | Models; cells | Fully open | Searchable database of standardized models whose properties have been measured with consistent virtual protocols. |
| **[Blue Brain Project open data & portals](https://portal.bluebrain.epfl.ch)** | EPFL Blue Brain Project (archived/transitioned 2024-) (Switzerland) | Rat; mouse; human | Reconstructed microcircuit models, Blue Brain Cell Atlas, morphologies, ion-channel data (Channelpedia) | Cells; circuits; models | Fully open, CC-BY | Digital reconstructions of neocortical microcircuitry, hippocampus and whole mouse-brain cell densities, plus the underlying morphology and electrophysiology datasets; distributed via the Blue Brain Open Data AWS bucket and EBRAINS. |
| **[The Virtual Brain (TVB)](https://www.thevirtualbrain.org)** | Charité Berlin / Baycrest / EBRAINS (Germany; Canada) | Human; mouse | Whole-brain network simulation from individual connectomes; TVB-EBRAINS cloud services | Models; circuits | Open source, GPL | Leading whole-brain simulation platform for personalized brain models, used in epilepsy surgery planning (Virtual Epileptic Patient) and large-scale dynamics research. |
| **[Human Neocortical Neurosolver (HNN)](https://hnn.brown.edu)** | Brown University (USA) | Human | Biophysically detailed cortical column model for interpreting MEG/EEG source signals | Models; circuits | Open source, BSD | Connects macroscale human electrophysiology to the cellular/circuit mechanisms that could generate it. |
| **[Allen Brain Modeling Toolkit & V1 model](https://portal.brain-map.org/explore/models)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Mouse | Biophysically detailed and point-neuron models of mouse visual cortex (~230,000 neurons); SONATA format | Models; circuits | Fully open | Large-scale data-driven cortical circuit model released with the BMTK simulation framework and the SONATA standard for network description. |
| **[OpenWorm](http://openworm.org)** | OpenWorm Foundation (distributed, volunteer-led) (International) | C. elegans | Whole-organism simulation: c302 neural model, Sibernetic body physics, cell-by-cell anatomy | Models; cells; circuits; behavior | Open source, MIT | The most advanced attempt at a complete in-silico organism, integrating connectome, neuron models and biomechanics with open community governance. |
| **[Brain-Score](https://www.brain-score.org)** | MIT (DiCarlo lab) / Quest for Intelligence (USA) | NHP; human | Benchmarks scoring artificial networks against primate neural recordings and behavior | Models; activity; behavior | Open source + open benchmarks | Standardized leaderboard comparing deep-network representations to ventral-stream neural responses and behavioral error patterns; extended to language with Brain-Score Language. |
| **[EBRAINS Model Catalog & simulation services](https://ebrains.eu/service/model-catalog)** | EBRAINS AISBL (Europe) | All | Model registry with validation tests; NEST, Arbor, TVB, NEURON on HPC | Models | Open; free account | Registry linking models to validation suites and to HPC resources, so published models can be re-run against data. |
| **[Neuroscience Gateway (NSG)](https://www.nsgportal.org)** | UC San Diego / SDSC (USA) | All | Free supercomputer access for NEURON, NEST, Brian, The Virtual Brain, EEG pipelines | Models | Free with academic account | Removes the compute barrier for large simulations and EEG processing by giving researchers free XSEDE/ACCESS allocations through a simple portal. |
| **[BioModels](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biomodels)** | EMBL-EBI (Europe) | All | Curated, annotated systems-biology models (SBML), incl. signaling and metabolic models relevant to neurons | Models; genes | Fully open, CC0 | Standards-based model repository with reproducibility curation, complementary to ModelDB's neuron-centric scope. |
| **[Open Brain Institute](https://www.openbraininstitute.org/)** | Open Brain Institute (non-profit, successor to EPFL Blue Brain Project) (Switzerland) | Mouse; rat | Cloud 'virtual labs' for building and simulating digital brain tissue | Models; cells; circuits | Open; account for virtual labs | Non-profit launched in March 2025 that took over the Blue Brain Project's models, algorithms and tooling and offers them as cloud virtual labs for building biologically detailed digital brain tissue. Now funded philanthropically and by users rather than by a federal mandate. |
