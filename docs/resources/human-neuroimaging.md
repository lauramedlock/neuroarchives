# Human neuroimaging cohorts & population studies

Human MRI and PET, from small frictionless method-development datasets to population cohorts of 100,000 participants.

*24 resources. Generated from [`data/neuroscience_data_resources.csv`](../about/contributing.md) — edit the CSV, not this page.*

| Resource | Host (Region) | Species | Data type / technique | Level | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[UK Biobank Imaging](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk)** | UK Biobank / University of Oxford (analysis) (UK) | Human | Multimodal brain MRI (T1, T2-FLAIR, swMRI, dMRI, rfMRI, tfMRI) in ~100,000 participants | Circuits; activity; genes; behavior | Approved-application access (fee) | The largest brain-imaging cohort in existence, linked to genotypes, health records and deep lifestyle phenotyping; derived imaging-derived phenotypes and GWAS summary statistics are widely reused. |
| **[ABCD Study](https://abcdstudy.org)** | NIH / 21 US sites; data via NDA (USA) | Human (ages 9-10 at baseline) | Longitudinal multimodal MRI, cognition, mental health, substance use, genetics | Activity; behavior; genes | Data Use Certification via NDA | Largest long-term study of child brain development (~11,800 youth) with annual releases through the NIMH Data Archive. |
| **[HEALthy Brain and Child Development (HBCD)](https://hbcdstudy.org)** | NIH HEAL Initiative / NDA (USA) | Human (pregnancy to early childhood) | Infant MRI, EEG, biospecimens, behavioral and environmental measures | Activity; behavior | Controlled access via NDA | Prospective cohort following brain development from pregnancy through early childhood, releasing data via NDA. |
| **[Baby Connectome Project (BCP)](https://nda.nih.gov/edit_collection.html?id=2848)** | UNC Chapel Hill / University of Minnesota (USA) | Human (0-5 years) | Longitudinal structural, diffusion and resting-state MRI in typically developing children | Circuits; activity | Controlled access via NDA | Dense early-childhood imaging resource distributed through the NIMH Data Archive. |
| **[ADNI](https://adni.loni.usc.edu)** | Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative / LONI (USA) | Human | Serial MRI, amyloid/tau PET, FDG-PET, CSF biomarkers, cognition, genetics | Activity; genes; behavior | Application-based access | The template for open biomarker cohorts in neurodegeneration, now in its fourth phase with multi-ethnic recruitment. |
| **[OASIS (OASIS-3 / OASIS-4)](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains)** | Washington University in St. Louis / Knight ADRC (USA) | Human | MRI, PET (PIB, AV45, FDG), clinical and cognitive data in aging and dementia | Activity; behavior | Free with data-use agreement | Longitudinal open dataset of normal aging and Alzheimer's disease with minimal access friction relative to comparable cohorts. |
| **[PPMI](https://www.ppmi-info.org)** | Michael J. Fox Foundation / LONI (USA; International) | Human | MRI, DaT-SPECT, biospecimens, genetics, longitudinal clinical assessment in Parkinson's | Activity; genes | Application-based access | Flagship Parkinson's biomarker cohort including prodromal and genetic-risk arms. |
| **[IXI Dataset](https://brain-development.org/ixi-dataset)** | Imperial College London / Brain Development project (UK) | Human | T1, T2, PD, MRA and diffusion MRI in ~600 healthy adults | Circuits | Fully open, CC-BY-SA | Small, entirely frictionless healthy-adult MRI dataset commonly used for method development and registration testing. |
| **[ABIDE I & II](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/abide)** | INDI / Child Mind Institute (multi-site) (International) | Human | Resting-state fMRI, structural MRI and phenotypes in autism and controls | Activity; behavior | Open (phenotypes partly restricted) | Aggregated autism neuroimaging across >24 sites; the most-used open dataset in clinical connectomics machine learning. |
| **[ADHD-200 & CoRR](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/adhd200)** | INDI / Child Mind Institute (International) | Human | Resting-state fMRI; test-retest reliability collections | Activity; behavior | Fully open | ADHD-200 provides a diagnostic-classification benchmark; the Consortium for Reliability and Reproducibility supplies multi-session retest data for measuring reliability. |
| **[Enhanced NKI-Rockland Sample](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/enhanced)** | Nathan Kline Institute (USA) | Human | Multiband resting/task fMRI, dMRI, deep psychiatric and physical phenotyping, lifespan 6-85 | Activity; behavior | Open with data-use agreement | Community-ascertained lifespan sample designed explicitly for discovery of brain-behavior relationships. |
| **[Natural Scenes Dataset (NSD)](https://naturalscenesdataset.org)** | University of Minnesota / Washington University (USA) | Human | 7T fMRI, 8 subjects viewing ~70,000 COCO images (30-40 scan sessions each) | Activity | Open with data-use terms | The largest high-resolution human visual fMRI dataset, built for training and testing encoding models of natural vision. |
| **[THINGS-data](https://things-initiative.org)** | Max Planck Institute / NIMH (Germany; USA) | Human | fMRI, MEG and behavioral similarity judgments over 1,854 object concepts | Activity; behavior | Fully open | Multimodal object-recognition resource combining large-scale neuroimaging with millions of behavioral triplet judgments. |
| **[BOLD5000](https://bold5000.github.io)** | Carnegie Mellon University (USA) | Human | fMRI responses to 5,000 natural scene images in 4 subjects | Activity | Fully open | Image-rich fMRI dataset bridging computer-vision benchmarks (ImageNet, COCO, SUN) and human visual cortex. |
| **[Algonauts Project](http://algonauts.csail.mit.edu)** | MIT / Freie Universität Berlin (USA; Germany) | Human | fMRI and MEG benchmark challenges for vision and video models | Activity; models | Fully open | Recurring challenge series that supplies held-out neural data for benchmarking computational models of biological vision. |
| **[Courtois NeuroMod](https://www.cneuromod.ca)** | CRIUGM / Université de Montréal (Canada) | Human | Very deep individual fMRI (100+ hours per subject) on movies, video games, speech | Activity; behavior | Open with registration | Extreme individual-level sampling designed for training subject-specific models of brain responses to naturalistic stimuli. |
| **[Individual Brain Charting (IBC)](https://project.inria.fr/IBC)** | NeuroSpin / Inria, CEA Saclay (France) | Human | Deep multi-task fMRI (>100 cognitive tasks) in 12 subjects | Activity; behavior | Fully open | Fine-grained functional atlas of cognition at the individual level, complementing group task batteries. |
| **[StudyForrest](https://www.studyforrest.org)** | Otto-von-Guericke University Magdeburg (Germany) | Human | 3T/7T fMRI during audio-movie and visual-movie stimulation, plus eye tracking and audio annotations | Activity; behavior | Fully open | Long-running naturalistic-stimulation resource with extensive derived annotations of the stimulus itself. |
| **[AOMIC](https://nilab-uva.github.io/AOMIC.github.io)** | University of Amsterdam (Netherlands) | Human | Three large BIDS cohorts (~1,500 subjects) of structural, diffusion and task/rest fMRI with psychometrics | Activity; behavior | Fully open | Fully preprocessed, well-documented population-level neuroimaging collections released on OpenNeuro. |
| **[MICA-MICs](https://portal.conp.ca/dataset?id=projects/mica-mics)** | McGill University / MNI (Canada) | Human | 7T and 3T multimodal connectomes incl. microstructure profiles | Circuits | Fully open | Micro-structurally informed connectomes for studying cortical hierarchies and gradients. |
| **[Neurosynth & NeuroQuery](https://neurosynth.org)** | University of Colorado Boulder; Inria (USA; France) | Human | Automated meta-analysis over >14,000 fMRI publications; coordinate and text models | Activity | Fully open | Generate meta-analytic brain maps from a term or a sentence; Neurosynth supplies coordinate-based meta-analysis, NeuroQuery predictive mapping. |
| **[ENIGMA Consortium](https://enigma.ini.usc.edu)** | University of Southern California (Thompson lab) + ~2,000 scientists (International) | Human | Harmonized imaging and genetic meta-analysis across >45 disorder working groups | Circuits; genes | Open summary data; membership for working groups | Shares summary statistics, effect-size maps and the ENIGMA Toolbox rather than raw data, enabling very large-N disorder comparisons. |
| **[Brain Genomics Superstruct Project (GSP)](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataverse/GSP)** | Harvard University / MGH (USA) | Human | Structural and resting-state MRI with cognition and personality in ~1,570 young adults | Activity; behavior | Open with data-use terms | Large single-site healthy young-adult sample released through Harvard Dataverse. |
| **[Chinese Color Nest Project (CCNP)](https://ccnp.scidb.cn)** | Institute of Psychology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (China) | Human | Accelerated-longitudinal lifespan MRI with behavior | Activity; behavior | Open with registration | Devoted to normative brain-development charting in a Chinese population, addressing the Western-sample bias of most open cohorts. |
