# Human electrophysiology (EEG / MEG / iEEG)

Non-invasive and intracranial human electrophysiology, including clinical corpora and brain-computer-interface benchmarks.

*8 resources. Generated from [`data/neuroscience_data_resources.csv`](../about/contributing.md) — edit the CSV, not this page.*

| Resource | Host (Region) | Species | Data type / technique | Level | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[Temple University Hospital EEG Corpus (TUEG)](https://isip.piconepress.com/projects/nedc)** | Temple University (USA) | Human | Clinical scalp EEG with reports; seizure, artifact and abnormality annotations | Activity | Free with signed agreement | The largest open clinical EEG archive — tens of thousands of recordings — with expert-annotated subsets for seizure detection and abnormal-EEG classification. |
| **[PhysioNet](https://physionet.org)** | MIT Laboratory for Computational Physiology (USA) | Human; animal | EEG, ECoG, polysomnography, EMG, physiologic waveforms | Activity | Open and credentialed tiers | Hosts benchmark neuro datasets such as CHB-MIT scalp EEG (pediatric seizures), Sleep-EDF and the EEG Motor Movement/Imagery database, with WFDB tooling. |
| **[Cam-CAN](https://cam-can.mrc-cbu.cam.ac.uk)** | MRC Cognition and Brain Sciences Unit, Cambridge (UK) | Human | MRI, MEG and extensive cognitive testing across 700 adults aged 18-88 | Activity; behavior | Application-based access | Population-based lifespan cohort designed for studying healthy cognitive ageing with matched structural, functional and MEG data. |
| **[OMEGA (Open MEG Archive)](https://www.mcgill.ca/bic/neuroinformatics/omega)** | McGill University / MNI (Canada) | Human | Resting-state MEG with matched MRI | Activity | Free registration | First open MEG repository, designed for resting-state analyses and distributed through the Brainstorm ecosystem. |
| **[MPI-Leipzig Mind-Brain-Body (LEMON)](https://www.nitrc.org/projects/mpilmbb)** | Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences (Germany) | Human | 64-channel resting EEG, MRI, extensive psychological and physiological phenotyping | Activity; behavior | Fully open | Deeply phenotyped young and older adult cohort combining EEG and MRI with questionnaires and bodily measures; released in BIDS on OpenNeuro/GWDG. |
| **[Healthy Brain Network (HBN)](https://healthybrainnetwork.org)** | Child Mind Institute (USA) | Human (children/adolescents) | MRI, high-density EEG, eye tracking, actigraphy, deep clinical phenotyping | Activity; behavior | Data-use agreement via NDA/FCP-INDI | Large transdiagnostic pediatric biobank (target >10,000) releasing imaging, EEG and clinical measures on a rolling basis. |
| **[MOABB / BNCI Horizon benchmarks](https://neurotechx.github.io/moabb)** | Inria / TU Graz and community (Europe) | Human | Motor imagery, P300, SSVEP BCI datasets with standardized evaluation | Activity; behavior | Open source, BSD | Mother of All BCI Benchmarks aggregates dozens of open BCI datasets behind one Python API for reproducible decoder comparison. |
| **[RAM / Computational Memory Lab datasets](https://memory.psych.upenn.edu/Electrophysiological_Data)** | University of Pennsylvania (Kahana lab) / DARPA RAM (USA) | Human | Intracranial EEG during memory tasks, including stimulation sessions (~300 patients) | Activity; behavior | Open with registration | Large human iEEG corpus for episodic memory with direct brain-stimulation experiments; distributed through the lab portal and openneuro. |
