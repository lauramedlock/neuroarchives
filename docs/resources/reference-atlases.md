# Reference atlases & coordinate frameworks

Reference spaces and anatomical frameworks. Registering your own data into one of these is usually the precondition for comparing it to anything else in this list.

*20 resources. Generated from [`data/neuroscience_data_resources.csv`](../about/contributing.md) — edit the CSV, not this page.*

| Resource | Host (Region) | Species | Data type / technique | Level | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[Allen Brain Map (portal)](https://portal.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Mouse; human; NHP | Umbrella portal: ISH, transcriptomics, connectivity, cell types, ephys, morphology | Genes; cells; circuits; activity | Fully open; Allen Terms of Use | Entry point to the Allen Institute's full suite of atlases and data products, with the AllenSDK Python client for programmatic access. |
| **[Allen Mouse Brain Atlas (ISH)](https://mouse.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Mouse | Genome-wide in situ hybridization, ~20,000 genes, cellular resolution | Genes | Fully open | The original whole-genome spatial expression atlas of the adult mouse brain, registered to a common 3D reference. |
| **[Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate Framework (CCFv3)](https://atlas.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Mouse | 3D average template + hierarchical anatomical ontology at 10 µm | Circuits | Fully open | The de facto standard mouse brain reference space; nearly all modern mouse datasets are registered into it. |
| **[Allen Human Brain Atlas](https://human.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Human | Microarray transcriptomics across ~900 anatomic sites in 6 donors; MRI; histology | Genes | Fully open | Anatomically comprehensive transcriptional map of the adult human brain; the basis for most imaging-transcriptomics work. |
| **[BrainSpan: Atlas of the Developing Human Brain](https://www.brainspan.org)** | Allen Institute / NIH BrainSpan consortium (USA) | Human | Bulk RNA-seq, exon arrays, ISH across prenatal-to-adult development | Genes | Fully open | Developmental transcriptome spanning 8 post-conception weeks to adulthood across cortical and subcortical regions. |
| **[Allen Developing Mouse Brain Atlas](https://developingmouse.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Mouse | ISH across 7 developmental stages | Genes | Fully open | Spatiotemporal gene-expression atlas of mouse brain development with a developmental ontology. |
| **[Allen Brain Cell (ABC) Atlas](https://portal.brain-map.org/atlases-and-data/bkp/abc-atlas)** | Allen Institute / BICCN / BICAN (USA) | Mouse; human; marmoset | Whole-brain single-cell transcriptomics + MERFISH spatial; cross-species taxonomies | Genes; cells | Fully open; AWS S3 mirror | Interactive whole-brain cell-type atlas: ~4M mouse cells in a hierarchical taxonomy plus spatially registered MERFISH sections; data also on AWS Open Data. |
| **[Brain Knowledge Platform / BCDC](https://knowledge.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Mouse; human; NHP | Index across cell-type, imaging and omics archives | Genes; cells | Fully open | Indexes BICCN/BICAN products held in NeMO, BossDB, BIL and DANDI, making cell-census data findable from one place. |
| **[Allen Cell Types Database](https://celltypes.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute for Brain Science (USA) | Mouse; human | Patch-clamp electrophysiology; biocytin morphology; Patch-seq; biophysical models | Cells; models | Fully open | Standardized single-neuron electrophysiology and morphology for cortical cell types, each with fitted biophysically detailed and GLIF models. |
| **[BigBrain](https://bigbrainproject.org)** | Jülich Research Centre & McGill (MNI) (Germany; Canada) | Human | 20 µm isotropic histological reconstruction of a single human brain | Cells; circuits | Fully open, CC-BY | Ultra-high-resolution cytoarchitectonic 3D model built from 7,404 histological sections; served via EBRAINS and the BigBrain Project. |
| **[Julich-Brain Cytoarchitectonic Atlas](https://ebrains.eu/service/human-brain-atlas)** | Forschungszentrum Jülich / EBRAINS (Germany) | Human | Probabilistic cytoarchitectonic maps in MNI space | Circuits | Fully open | Probability maps of ~250 cortical and subcortical areas derived from 10 post-mortem brains, with receptor-density and connectivity overlays. |
| **[Waxholm Space Atlas of the Rat Brain](https://www.nitrc.org/projects/whs-sd-atlas)** | NTNU / University of Oslo / EBRAINS (Norway) | Rat | MRI/DTI-based 3D reference with delineated regions | Circuits | Fully open | The standard rat brain coordinate space and segmentation, continuously extended with new region delineations. |
| **[BrainGlobe Atlas API](https://brainglobe.info)** | Sainsbury Wellcome Centre / UCL (UK) | Mouse; rat; zebrafish; human; fly; NHP; others | Unified Python access to >30 reference atlases | Circuits | Open source, BSD | Single Python interface that downloads and harmonizes brain atlases across species, with registration (brainreg) and cell-detection tooling. |
| **[Scalable Brain Atlas](https://scalablebrainatlas.incf.org)** | Netherlands Institute for Neuroscience (Netherlands) | Macaque; marmoset; mouse; rat; human | Web-based atlas templates and coordinate conversion | Circuits | Fully open | Browser-based viewer and services for comparing delineations across species and atlas versions. |
| **[TemplateFlow](https://www.templateflow.org)** | McGill / Stanford (NiPreps) (Canada; USA) | Human; rodent; NHP | Archive of neuroimaging templates and spatial mappings | Circuits | Open, CC-BY | Versioned, programmatically accessible library of standard brain templates used by fMRIPrep and related pipelines. |
| **[BALSA](https://balsa.wustl.edu)** | Washington University in St. Louis (USA) | Human; macaque | Surface-based scenes, parcellations (HCP-MMP1.0), connectivity maps | Circuits | Open; account for download | Repository for surface-based neuroimaging scenes and the multi-modal cortical parcellation used throughout HCP analyses. |
| **[Brainnetome Atlas](https://atlas.brainnetome.org)** | Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (China) | Human; macaque | Connectivity-based parcellation (246 regions) with functional/structural profiles | Circuits | Fully open | In-vivo connectivity-derived human brain parcellation, widely used as an alternative to cytoarchitectonic atlases. |
| **[Human Protein Atlas — Brain](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/brain)** | KTH / SciLifeLab (Sweden) | Human; mouse; pig | Immunohistochemistry; bulk and single-cell transcriptomics by region | Genes; cells | Fully open, CC-BY-SA | Protein- and transcript-level expression across brain regions with cross-species comparison and antibody-validated imaging. |
| **[Allen Aging, Dementia and TBI Study](https://aging.brain-map.org)** | Allen Institute / UW / Kaiser Permanente (ACT cohort) (USA) | Human | Bulk RNA-seq, protein quantification, neuropathology in aged donors | Genes | Fully open | Deeply phenotyped aged-brain resource linking TBI exposure, dementia status and molecular measures. |
| **[SEA-AD (Seattle Alzheimer's Disease Brain Cell Atlas)](https://sea-ad.org)** | Allen Institute / UW / Kaiser Permanente (USA) | Human | snRNA-seq; snATAC-seq; MERFISH; quantitative neuropathology imaging | Genes; cells | Fully open | Single-cell atlas of middle temporal gyrus and DLPFC across the full Alzheimer's severity continuum, with matched quantitative pathology. |
