# Disease, genetics & biobank portals

Disease cohorts, genetic association resources and the post-mortem tissue banks behind most human molecular neuroscience.

*11 resources. Generated from [`data/neuroscience_data_resources.csv`](../about/contributing.md) — edit the CSV, not this page.*

| Resource | Host (Region) | Species | Data type / technique | Level | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[AD Knowledge Portal](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org)** | Sage Bionetworks / NIA (AMP-AD) (USA) | Human; mouse models | Multi-omics (RNA-seq, proteomics, metabolomics, snRNA-seq), imaging, clinical; model-AD mouse data | Genes; cells | Open + controlled tiers | Central hub for NIA-funded Alzheimer's systems-biology data across many cohorts (ROSMAP, MSBB, Mayo) with harmonized metadata. |
| **[AMP PD](https://amp-pd.org)** | NIH Accelerating Medicines Partnership - Parkinson's Disease (USA) | Human | Harmonized whole-genome sequencing, transcriptomics, proteomics and clinical data across PD cohorts | Genes | Credentialed cloud access | Unified analysis platform joining PPMI, PDBP and other cohorts in a cloud workspace. |
| **[Psychiatric Genomics Consortium (PGC)](https://pgc.unc.edu)** | PGC (international; coordination at UNC/Broad) (International) | Human | GWAS summary statistics for schizophrenia, bipolar, depression, ADHD, autism and more | Genes | Open summary statistics | The largest psychiatric-genetics meta-analyses; summary statistics are freely downloadable and underpin most polygenic-score work in psychiatry. |
| **[SFARI Base / SPARK](https://base.sfari.org)** | Simons Foundation Autism Research Initiative (USA) | Human | Genomic, phenotypic and longitudinal data from >100,000 autistic individuals and families | Genes; behavior | Application-based access | The largest autism genetic cohort, with SFARI Gene providing curated gene-evidence scoring. |
| **[Autism BrainNet](https://autismbrainnet.org)** | Simons Foundation (USA) | Human | Post-mortem brain tissue from autistic donors and controls | Cells; genes | Tissue request by application | Tissue-request network (not a data download) enabling molecular and anatomical studies of autism brain. |
| **[NIH NeuroBioBank](https://neurobiobank.nih.gov)** | NIH (NIMH/NINDS/NICHD) with six US brain banks (USA) | Human | Post-mortem brain tissue across neurological and psychiatric diagnoses | Cells; genes | Tissue request by application | Federated US brain-tissue repository with a single request portal and standardized collection protocols. |
| **[Netherlands Brain Bank](https://www.brainbank.nl)** | Netherlands Institute for Neuroscience (KNAW) (Netherlands) | Human | Post-mortem tissue with detailed clinical records and short post-mortem delay | Cells | Tissue request by application | One of the longest-running and most clinically documented brain-tissue collections worldwide. |
| **[Open Targets Platform & Genetics](https://platform.opentargets.org)** | EMBL-EBI / Wellcome Sanger / GSK and partners (Europe) | Human | Target-disease association evidence integrating GWAS, expression, genetics, literature | Genes | Fully open | Useful for moving from a neuro-GWAS locus to candidate genes with supporting functional evidence. |
| **[GWAS Catalog](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas)** | EMBL-EBI / NHGRI (Europe; USA) | Human | Curated published GWAS associations and summary statistics | Genes | Fully open | Standardized catalog where most brain-trait and neuropsychiatric GWAS results are indexed and downloadable. |
| **[dbGaP](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gap)** | NCBI / NIH (USA) | Human | Controlled-access genotype-phenotype data for hundreds of neuro cohorts | Genes; behavior | Controlled access | The gatekeeper for individual-level human genetic data underlying many open summary-level neuro resources. |
| **[Korea Brain Bank Network](https://k-brainnet.kbri.re.kr/eng/home/index.do)** | Korea Brain Research Institute (KBRI) (South Korea) | Human | Post-mortem brain tissue collection and distribution across a national bank network | Cells; genes | Tissue request by application | National brain-tissue network established under the Korea Brain Initiative, coordinating collection and distribution across Korean brain banks. Tissue is requested through the network portal. |
