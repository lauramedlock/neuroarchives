# Model-organism knowledgebases

The per-species knowledgebases that connect genes and genetic reagents to nervous-system anatomy.

*7 resources. Generated from [`data/neuroscience_data_resources.csv`](../about/contributing.md) — edit the CSV, not this page.*

| Resource | Host (Region) | Species | Data type / technique | Level | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[WormBase](https://wormbase.org)** | WormBase consortium (Caltech, EMBL-EBI, OICR, WUSTL) (International) | C. elegans and other nematodes | Genome annotation, gene function, expression, phenotypes, neuron-level anatomy ontology | Genes; cells | Fully open | Central knowledgebase for nematode biology, including the anatomy ontology that names every neuron and its connections. |
| **[WormAtlas](https://www.wormatlas.org)** | WormAtlas consortium (Albert Einstein College of Medicine and others) (USA) | C. elegans | Curated anatomical handbooks, EM image archives, neuron-by-neuron descriptions | Cells; circuits | Fully open | Definitive descriptive anatomy of C. elegans, including the original EM plates and the neuronal wiring handbooks. |
| **[FlyBase](https://flybase.org)** | FlyBase consortium (Harvard, Cambridge, Indiana, UNM) (USA; UK) | Drosophila melanogaster | Genes, alleles, transgenic constructs, expression, phenotypes, literature curation | Genes | Fully open | The genetic and genomic reference for Drosophila, essential for mapping connectome cell types onto driver lines and genotypes. |
| **[ZFIN](https://zfin.org)** | University of Oregon (USA) | Zebrafish | Gene expression, mutants, transgenic lines, anatomy ontology, phenotypes | Genes; cells | Fully open | The zebrafish model-organism database, linking genetic reagents to nervous-system anatomy and phenotype. |
| **[Mouse Genome Informatics (MGI)](https://www.informatics.jax.org)** | The Jackson Laboratory (USA) | Mouse | Genes, alleles, strains, expression (GXD), phenotype and disease models | Genes; behavior | Fully open | Authoritative mouse genetics resource; the Gene Expression Database (GXD) covers developmental CNS expression in detail. |
| **[Rat Genome Database (RGD)](https://rgd.mcw.edu)** | Medical College of Wisconsin (USA) | Rat | Genes, QTLs, strains, phenotypes, disease annotations | Genes; behavior | Fully open | Primary rat knowledgebase, including neurobehavioral QTL and strain-phenotype data complementary to the Waxholm atlas. |
| **[ZEBrA (Zebra finch Expression Brain Atlas)](http://www.zebrafinchatlas.org)** | Oregon Health & Science University (USA) | Zebra finch | ISH gene-expression atlas of the songbird brain | Genes; circuits | Fully open | Expression atlas for the premier vocal-learning model, mapping genes onto song-control nuclei. |
