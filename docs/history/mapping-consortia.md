# Atlases, connectomes and large-scale mapping consortia

These projects produce the reference maps — which cells exist, where they are, and how they connect — that any unified brain model needs. The mouse now has a near-complete cell atlas and a fully mapped cubic millimetre; the fly has a complete connectome; human and primate atlases are the 2026–2030 frontier.

## Allen Institute for Brain Science (2003–present)

- **Proposed:** Founded by Paul Allen to produce large, standardized, fully open brain datasets.
- **Created:** The Allen Mouse Brain Atlas (2006), Human Brain Atlas, Mouse Connectivity Atlas, Brain Observatory, and the [brain-map.org](https://brain-map.org/consortia/bican) portal; lead institution for BICCN, BICAN and MICrONS. Its individual data products are listed under [reference atlases](../resources/reference-atlases.md), [connectomics](../resources/connectomics.md) and [neurophysiology](../resources/neurophysiology.md).
- **Status:** Active; the anchor data producer for the US cell-atlas effort.

## Human Connectome Project (2009–present)

- **Proposed:** An NIH Blueprint project to map human brain connectivity in 1,200 healthy adults with advanced MRI.
- **Created:** Open imaging data, the HCP processing pipelines and multimodal cortical parcellation, later extended to Lifespan (development and aging) and disease cohorts.
- **Status:** The original project is complete; its data, pipelines and follow-on cohorts remain heavily used and are now hosted through NIH data archives ([ConnectomeDB and the HCP-Lifespan cohorts](../resources/connectomics.md)).

## BICCN → BICAN (2014–present)

- **Proposed:** A census of every brain cell type, first in mouse (BICCN, 2017–2022), then human, macaque and marmoset across the lifespan (BICAN).
- **Created:** A whole-mouse-brain cell atlas (published 2023) and, from BICAN, [11 grants worth \~$500 million over 5 years](https://www.genomeweb.com/node/591711), led by the Allen Institute. BICAN released data in [October 2024 and May 2026](https://brain-map.org/consortia/bican), and a multispecies [basal ganglia atlas in 2026](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.14.717814.full.pdf).
- **Status:** Active through 2027. NIH has [re-issued the BICAN data coordinating unit](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/funding/grant-writing-and-application-process/concept-clearances/2026/brain-cell-atlas-from-data-to-knowledge-base) for awards starting July 2027, aiming at a permanent "Brain Cell Atlas Knowledge Base."

## Allen Brain Knowledge Platform (2020s–present)

- **Proposed:** A single open, online home where researchers can find, explore and analyze brain cell-atlas data across species, from planning an experiment to analyzing its results.
- **Created:** The [Brain Knowledge Platform](https://brain-map.org/bkp) on brain-map.org, combining BICCN, BICAN and ASAP data for mouse, human and macaque. Its tools include a data catalog, the ABC Atlas (cell types and anatomy), MapMyCells (maps a lab's own cells onto reference cell types), the Cell Type Knowledge Explorer, a Genetic Tools Atlas, lab protocols and programming interfaces.
- **Status:** Active and live. Human and mouse are well covered; several macaque regions, such as the cerebellum and hypothalamus, have no data listed yet. Full entry: [Brain Knowledge Platform / BCDC](../resources/reference-atlases.md).
- **Outlook:** It is the natural public front end for BICAN's atlases and NIH's planned Brain Cell Atlas Knowledge Base, so its growth tracks BICAN funding beyond 2027.

## BRAIN CONNECTS (2023–present)

- **Proposed:** Tools to map wiring across whole brains, at synaptic and mesoscale resolution, in mouse, primate and human.
- **Created:** [11 projects totalling $150 million over 5 years](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.15745), including a [10 mm³ mouse hippocampus connectome](https://www.highergov.com/grant/UM1NS132250) (Harvard, Google) as a proof of feasibility for a whole mouse brain.
- **Status:** Active; new technology calls are [open into 2027](https://research.njit.edu/node/19036).

## MICrONS (2016–2025)

- **Proposed:** An IARPA program to map the function and full wiring of one cubic millimetre of mouse visual cortex, to inspire better machine learning.
- **Created:** Activity from \~75,000 neurons plus an electron-microscopy wiring diagram of [200,000+ cells and half a billion synapses](https://alleninstitute.org/news/revealing-the-largest-wiring-diagram-and-functional-map-of-the-brain-through-microns), published as 10 Nature-family papers in April 2025, with open data on [MICrONS Explorer](../resources/connectomics.md).
- **Status:** Program complete; the dataset is now a benchmark for brain foundation models (see below).

## FlyWire and fly connectomics (2019–present)

- **Proposed:** A community, AI-assisted reconstruction of the adult fruit-fly brain from electron microscopy (Princeton-led, building on Janelia's hemibrain).
- **Created:** The first complete adult fly brain connectome (\~140,000 neurons), published in Nature in 2024 and openly browsable through [FlyWire Codex](../resources/connectomics.md). Nature Methods named EM connectomics its Method of the Year 2025.
- **Status:** Active; work has moved to the male fly brain and ventral nerve cord, and to whole-body fly models.

## Blue Brain Project → Open Brain Institute (2005–present)

- **Proposed:** EPFL's Swiss national project to build biologically detailed digital reconstructions and simulations of the mouse brain.
- **Created:** Algorithms and models totalling [18 million lines of code](https://bbp.epfl.ch/bbp/research/domains/bluebrain/index.html), detailed neocortical and whole-mouse-brain models, and \~300 papers.
- **Status:** Blue Brain [ended in December 2024](https://bbp.epfl.ch/bbp/research/domains/bluebrain/index.html). Its assets moved to the non-profit [Open Brain Institute](https://www.openbraininstitute.org/), launched March 2025, which offers cloud "virtual labs" for building digital brain tissue; the Blue Brain models themselves remain downloadable ([computational models](../resources/models.md)).
- **Outlook:** Now dependent on philanthropic and user funding rather than a federal mandate.

## China mesoscale and primate mapping (2019–present)

- **Proposed:** A Mesoscopic Brain Connectome Project, initiated by Mu-ming Poo and Qingming Luo, to map cell types and connections across whole macaque and human brains.
- **Created:** [Ten atlas papers](https://cebsit.cas.cn/xwen/mtsm/2019n/202507/t20250718_7892485.html) in Cell journals (July 2025) from 300+ scientists; a China Brain Multi-omics Atlas of 1,000+ human brains is under way.
- **Status:** Scaling up via the ICPBM consortium launched September 2025 (see national section).
