# Data Archives, Standards and Infrastructure

The archiving layer is the most durable part of the field: nearly every archive below is still running, and shared formats (BIDS for imaging, NWB for neurophysiology) are what make pooled training data for brain models possible. Their main risk is funding that comes in short grant cycles.

!!! note "What this page does and does not contain"
    This table carries the **historical** facts — when each archive or standard started, who founded it, and where its
    funding stands as of October 2026. It deliberately omits what each one holds and how to get at it, because that
    information lives once, in the [resource list](../resources/index.md), with species, technique, biological level and
    access terms. Follow the right-hand column for the full entry.

| Resource | Started | Origin and historical role | Status (Oct 2026) | Full entry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| INCF (International Neuroinformatics Coordinating Facility) | 2005 | Founded by seven OECD countries; see the [full entry](national-initiatives.md) under coordination bodies. | Active; [co-hosted by KTH Stockholm](https://neurotecheu.umh.es/en/?p=1741), member-funded | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/standards-catalogs.md) |
| Brain Imaging Data Structure (BIDS) | 2016 | [Published in 2016](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4978148/) as a folder-and-metadata convention for MRI; community extensions for EEG, MEG, PET and microscopy followed. | Active; the de facto standard for human imaging | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/standards-catalogs.md) |
| Neurodata Without Borders (NWB) | 2014 (v2.0 in 2019) | [Started](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.04317) by the Kavli Foundation, the Allen Institute and Berkeley Lab to give neurophysiology what BIDS gave imaging. | Active; required format for DANDI | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/standards-catalogs.md) |
| OpenNeuro | 2017 (from OpenfMRI, 2010) | Grew out of OpenfMRI and became the [BRAIN-designated archive](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/funding/grant-writing-and-application-process/concept-clearances/2020/brain-initiative-data-archives-for-brain-initiative) for human imaging; the NEMAR and PET portals were added later. | Active; tied to BRAIN archive funding | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/federated-archives.md) |
| DANDI | 2020 | [BRAIN- and Simons-funded](https://www.braininitiative.org/?p=33393) archive built on NWB, with cloud analysis attached from the start. | Active; [hundreds of datasets, hundreds of terabytes](https://odin.mit.edu/odin.html) | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/federated-archives.md) |
| BRAIN archive network — NeMO, BIL, DABI, BossDB, EMBER | 2018–2025 | NIH chose one domain archive per data type rather than one archive for everything, and [made deposition a condition of BRAIN funding](https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/sharing-policies/data-repository-requirements/brain). | Active; BRAIN grantees must deposit by data type | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/federated-archives.md) |
| EBRAINS | 2019 (from the Human Brain Project) | Created as the HBP's legacy infrastructure after the 2014 governance overhaul redirected the flagship from simulation toward shared services. | Active; EBRAINS 2.0 funded to 2026, becoming a permanent European consortium | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/federated-archives.md) |
| Brain Knowledge Platform (BKP), Allen Institute | 2020s | Allen Institute front end assembling BICCN, BICAN and ASAP data into one catalog ([history entry](mapping-consortia.md)). | Active; human and mouse well covered, several macaque regions not yet | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/reference-atlases.md) |
| Canadian Open Neuroscience Platform (CONP) | 2017 ($10.17M awarded 2018) | Federated national portal linking 15 universities' data and tools, plus the NeuroLibre reproducible-publishing platform. | First phase [complete](https://braincanada.ca/funded-grants/canadian-open-neuroscience-platform-conp-1); portal [still online](https://portal.conp.ca/) with 150+ datasets; [paper in PLoS Comp Biol](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011230) | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/federated-archives.md) |
| Federated Research Data Repository (FRDR), Canada | c. 2019 | Canada's general-purpose national repository, built so that data without a domain archive still has a curated home and a funder-compliant deposit route. | Active; run by the [Digital Research Alliance of Canada](https://alliancecan.ca/en/services/research-data-management/frdr) | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/federated-archives.md) |
| UK Biobank imaging | 2014 | Extended the 500,000-volunteer cohort with brain and body MRI of 100,000 participants linked to genetics and health records. | [100,000th scan done July 2025](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/news/record-breaking-human-imaging-project-crosses-the-finish-line); [repeat scans of 60,000 by 2029](https://becarispublishing.com/doi/10.5555/e484e596-7fd5-4dcd-aa97-32328f02fa9c) | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/human-neuroimaging.md) |
| ENIGMA Consortium | 2009 | Meta-analysis network formed to pool imaging and genetics across sites instead of centralizing raw data. | Active; [1,400+ scientists in 43 countries, 50+ working groups](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/handle/1887/3134470) (as of 2020) | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/human-neuroimaging.md) |
| International Brain Laboratory | 2017 | 20+ labs agreed to run a single standardized mouse decision task so that recordings would compose into one brain-wide map. | Active; [brain-wide map published 2025](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2025/sep/complete-brain-activity-map-revealed-first-time) | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/neurophysiology.md) |
| ModelDB | 1996 (Yale SenseLab) | The first archive of published computational neuroscience models, and still the main one. | Active; ~1,900 models, new 2026 entries | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/models.md) |
| NeuroMorpho.Org | 2006 | Assembled from reconstructions contributed by hundreds of labs; a key input for detailed models. | Active; [80,000+ reconstructions by 2017](https://www.mbfbioscience.com/neuromorpho-org-releases-10000-neuron-reconstructions/), growing since | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/connectomics.md) |
| Open Source Brain and NeuroML | 2011 (NeuroML from the early 2000s) | NeuroML standardized how models are described; Open Source Brain added browser-based viewing, running and development on top of it. EBRAINS' Model Catalog plays the same role in Europe. | Active; [OSB v2](https://lists.cnsorg.org/hyperkitty/list/comp-neuro@lists.cnsorg.org/thread/H55MUZ3JYZ55KEL6SR2F7WVUPH4OW4LR/) in active development | [:material-arrow-right: resource list](../resources/models.md) |
