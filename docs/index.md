# Brain Data: A Field Guide to Neuroscience Resources

*Created October 2026 · @Laura M*

Two things sit behind this site. The first is a **history**: over three decades, about 30 major initiatives have tried
to pool brain data and build a shared model of the brain, and as of October 2026 most survive as permanent archives,
atlases and AI models rather than as the original megaprojects. The second is the **standing inventory** those
initiatives left behind — 188 open resources across 13 categories, spanning *C. elegans* to human, molecules to
behaviour, each with its host, species, technique, biological level and access terms.

Read the history first if you want to know why the field looks the way it does; go straight to the resource list if you
are looking for data.

## Three things stand out

- **The data layer works.** Open archives (OpenNeuro, DANDI, EBRAINS) and shared formats (BIDS, NWB) are active and
  growing, and now supply training data for AI models.
- **The megaprojects have mostly converted.** The EU Human Brain Project became EBRAINS; Blue Brain became the Open
  Brain Institute; Japan's Brain/MINDS became Brain/MINDS 2.0.
- **The money is moving.** US BRAIN funding is down from its 2023 peak and faces a cliff after FY2026, while China and
  Japan are expanding; Canada and Australia still have no dedicated national program.

## How the two halves fit together

Many things appear in both halves of this site — EBRAINS is both a 2019 EU initiative and an archive you can download
from today. The split is by question:

| Question | Where it is answered |
|---|---|
| When did it start, who funded it, is it still funded? | [History](history/index.md) |
| What does it hold, for which species, and how do I get access? | [Resource list](resources/index.md) |

Descriptions are not repeated across the two: the history pages carry dates, budgets and status and link out to the
full entry, which carries species, technique, level and access. See [methodology](about/methodology.md) for how the two
source documents were merged.
